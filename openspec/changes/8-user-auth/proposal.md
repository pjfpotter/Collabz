# Proposal: Slice 1, sign-up and sign-in (#8)

## Why

The live test puts real students' data into Collabz, so every account has to be a real person who chose their course and agreed to how their data is used. Today the only way to be "signed in" is the foundation's dev switcher (#7), which lets anyone become any pretend user and doesn't exist on production. This slice gives production its front door: scan a QR code, sign up with an email magic link, pick a course, accept the terms, and sign in and out again later. It is track 1's first slice in the slice map (#18) and depends only on the foundation.

## What Changes

- **A `/signup` page with one form:** email, course (picked from the course list), and one tick box accepting the Terms & Conditions and the data policy. Nothing is sent until all three are filled in. The form then emails a magic link.
- **Course and terms travel inside the magic link**, and a `/signup/finish` step saves them when the link is opened. *Why:* on a phone, the link in Gmail often opens in a different browser from the one that scanned the QR code, so anything remembered in a cookie would be lost. An account with no course or no accepted terms counts as **not created**: it can't reach any other page until the finish step is done.
- **A `/signin` page** for people who already have an account: enter your email, get a magic link. Signing in with an email that has no account takes you to the finish step to pick a course and accept the terms first.
- **Sign-out from any page**, from a small "signed in as… / Sign out" control in the shared nav bar.
- **Suspended users can't sign in.** The sign-in is refused with a plain message, and a suspended user who is already signed in is treated as signed out.
- **First admins from `ADMIN_EMAILS`:** an email listed in that environment variable gets the admin role when its account is created. No email is committed to the repo.
- **`/terms` and `/privacy` pages**, readable without signing in, linked from the sign-up form. They cover what we hold (email, course, picks, photo, messages), who can see it, and that it is deleted by the admins at the end of the course.
- **`getCurrentUser()` returns the real signed-in user.** Its name and what it returns don't change, so no other track has to change anything. **Where the pretend cohort is switched on, the dev switcher stays as a fallback until join-up (#17)**, so teammates can keep testing as pretend users, who can't receive real email.
- **Magic-link emails are sent through Resend** from our own verified domain, so they don't land in spam.
- **A printable QR code** pointing at the live URL, generated by a small script.
- **New dependencies:** `next-auth@5` (Auth.js v5, still published as a beta), `@auth/prisma-adapter`, and `qrcode` (dev only, for the QR script).

### Decisions made for this change (asked of Tom, 8 Oct 2026)

| Question | Decision |
|---|---|
| Which sign-in library and version? | Auth.js, matching the project: the stack names "Auth.js" and the foundation copied its tables from the Auth.js Prisma adapter docs. That is **v5** (`next-auth@5` + `@auth/prisma-adapter`). v5 is still labelled beta, and the Prisma adapter expects the client at `@prisma/client` while ours is generated into `src/generated/prisma` (Prisma 7), so the first task proves the two work together before anything else is built |
| What happens to the dev switcher when this merges? | It stays, but only as a fallback where the pretend cohort is switched on (never production). Join-up (#17) removes it |
| How does sign-up survive the link opening in another browser? | Course and terms ride inside the magic link, and a finish step saves them. An account without them can't reach anything else |
| Email service (open question on #8) | Resend, sending from a domain we own and verify |
| Limit sign-up to particular email domains? (open question on #8) | No. Anyone with the QR code can sign up with any email. Admins can suspend anyone who shouldn't be there |
| When is live-test data deleted, and by whom? (open question on #8) | At the end of the course, by the admins (Patrick and Tom). **The exact date still has to be filled in** (see Open Questions in `design.md`) |

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `user-auth`: sign-up becomes one form (email, course, terms) finished by a step that works in any browser; an account with no course or terms counts as not created; sign-in refuses suspended users; adds the Terms & Conditions and data policy pages, first admins from `ADMIN_EMAILS`, and single-use, expiring magic links.

## Impact

- **Builds on #7 (foundation), merged to `master` on 8 Oct 2026 (PR #25).** Checked against what was built: the `User`, `Account`, `Session` and `VerificationToken` tables, `User.courseId` and `User.acceptedTermsAt` being empty-able, `getCurrentUser()` and `findPretendUser()` in `src/lib/currentUser.ts`, `isPretendCohortEnabled()` in `src/lib/pretendCohort.ts`, the nav bar, and the `/signup` and `/signin` placeholders all exist as this plan assumes. One difference from Auth.js's own schema matters here: our `User.email` is required and there are no `name` or `image` columns, so task 1.3 checks Auth.js doesn't try to write them.
- **For Patrick (#7), before the foundation is archived:** the `pretend-cohort` spec says the switcher lasts "until slice 1 (#8) replaces it". With the decision above it lasts until join-up (#17). That one line should change in #7's spec. Nothing else in #7 changes.
- **Schema:** no change planned. Task 1 checks the foundation's Auth.js tables against what `@auth/prisma-adapter` actually writes. If they don't fit, the fix goes to Patrick as an announced migration, not into this slice.
- **Code (new):** `src/auth.ts` (Auth.js set-up), `src/app/api/auth/[...nextauth]/route.ts`, `src/app/signup/` (form and finish step), `src/app/signin/`, `src/app/terms/`, `src/app/privacy/`, `src/lib/auth/` (sign-up checks, admin list, sending the email), `src/components/SignedInStatus.tsx`, `scripts/make-qr-code.ts`.
- **Shared code changed:** `getCurrentUser()` in `src/lib/currentUser.ts` (inside only, same name and return value), and **one line in the foundation's `NavBar.tsx`** to show `SignedInStatus`. Both are flagged to Patrick in the PR.
- **Routes owned:** `/signup` (and `/signup/finish`), `/signin`, `/terms`, `/privacy`, and `/api/auth/*`. The last three aren't in #7's route plan. They are in #8's scope (legal pages, and the address Auth.js needs), and nobody else plans to use those names.
- **Environment variables (new):** `AUTH_SECRET`, `AUTH_RESEND_KEY`, `EMAIL_FROM`, `ADMIN_EMAILS`, set locally, in Vercel Preview and in Vercel Production.
- **Outside the repo:** a Resend account, and DNS records on our domain so Resend can send from it.
- **Out of scope:** onboarding (slice 2), the account page and changing course later (slice 7), admin screens and suspending people (slice 8), deleting your own account (slice 7), and removing the switcher and pretend data (join-up, #17).
